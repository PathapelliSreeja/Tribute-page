Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

"The Missile Man of India and the People's President"

Biography

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists, teachers, and leaders. Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he came from a humble family and worked hard to achieve academic excellence. His dedication and determination inspired millions across the country.

Kalam played an important role in India's space and missile development programs. His contributions to ISRO and DRDO earned him the title "Missile Man of India." He was deeply committed to strengthening the nation's scientific and technological capabilities.

From 2002 to 2007, he served as the 11th President of India. Unlike many political leaders, he was admired for his simplicity, honesty, and close connection with students. He believed that young minds had the power to transform the future of the nation.

Even after completing his presidency, Dr. Kalam continued teaching, writing books, and motivating students until his final day. His life remains a shining example of hard work, humility, patriotism, and lifelong learning.

Major Achievements
